What a sponsor gets

Every issue we publish carries a sponsor, so this is the whole history. Each dot is one issue. The scale is how the issue's clicks divided up, not a click-through rate: a dot at 20% means one click in five that week went to the sponsor, against roughly twenty competing editorial links. The dashed line is the median, 1 in 9.

Share is measured against every editorial and sponsor link in that issue, excluding Workspace6's own navigation and unsubscribe links. Where a sponsor ran several links in one issue (a homepage, a blog post and a landing page, say) all of them are counted together.

Who sponsors us

Sponsors cluster in the tools DTC operators actually buy. Influencer and UGC platforms, email and SMS, and analytics lead, which tracks with what the audience runs day to day.

The sponsors who came back

Repeat bookings are the honest measure of whether sponsorship works here. These are the sponsors who booked again, most-run first. We publish how sponsorship performs across all issues, never how any individual sponsor did: those results belong to them.

Sponsor per issue
Read from each issue: the hero image's link, the "in partnership with" credit, and links carrying a Workspace6 campaign tag. App-store listings and hosted landing pages are attributed to the sponsor named in the credit, not to Shopify or HubSpot. Every one of the 177 published issues resolves to a sponsor, which matches the editorial rule that an issue does not go out without one.
Share of the issue's clicks
How an issue's clicks divided up: sponsor clicks against all editorial and sponsor clicks in that issue, excluding Workspace6's own navigation and unsubscribe links. Shown as a ratio rather than a percentage, because a percentage beside the word clicks is easily read as a click-through rate, which this is not. Issues with fewer than 25 outbound clicks are excluded because a handful of clicks produces a meaningless ratio. Figures are aggregated across all issues; no individual sponsor's results are published.

Member location
